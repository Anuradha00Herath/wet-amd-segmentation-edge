--------------------------------------------------------------------
  METRIC DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Precision  : TP / (TP+FP)  — how many predictions are correct
  Recall     : TP / (TP+FN)  — how many GT boxes were found
  F1         : harmonic mean of Precision and Recall
  mAP50      : mean AP at IoU≥0.50  (primary YOLO metric)
  mAP50-95   : mean AP averaged over IoU 0.50:0.05:0.95
  Mean IoU   : average IoU of matched box pairs
  Dice       : 2·IoU/(1+IoU)  — box-overlap F1 equivalent
  GFLOPs     : giga floating-point operations per image
  Latency    : mean over 100 runs, CUDA Events (GPU) or perf_counter (CPU)
